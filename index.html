04 — A FEW NOTES

Good to know.

A little clarity before you head out the door.

Do I need to go somewhere special?

No. A detour can happen in your neighborhood, at home, on the way to class, or during a break. The idea is simply to notice something differently.

Does this connect to an AI or track my location?

No. This is a lightweight interactive demo. The suggestions are built into the page, and it doesn’t ask for your location or connect to an AI service.

What if the suggestion doesn’t fit my day?

Try another idea, adapt it, or save it for later. A good plan should fit your day—not the other way around.

Can I do a detour with a friend?

Absolutely. Take turns choosing a direction, let each person add one small twist, and see where your shared curiosity goes.

Ready for a little change of scenery?
